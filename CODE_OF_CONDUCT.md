# Code of conduct

Hemma is made for households, by people who'd like their own home's page to be friendly. The same
goes for working on it together.

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different experience, languages and amounts of time.
  Assume good intent, explain instead of scolding, and say thanks.
- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Disagree with an idea or a change, never with someone's
  worth. Accept that a decision may go another way, and that it can be revisited with new reasons.
- **No harassment.** No insults, no demeaning jokes, no unwelcome attention, no sharing of anyone's
  private details (a home's addresses, names or logs included).
- **Mind the households.** Logs and screenshots in issues can show real homes: share only what you'd
  be happy for anyone to see, and help others do the same.

This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else the project is discussed.

If something isn't right, contact the maintainer privately - through the repository's
**Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability") if you'd rather it not be public. The maintainer will
listen, may ask what happened, and can edit or remove comments and block people who don't keep to
this. Reports are kept confidential.
